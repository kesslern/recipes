# Red Curry Lentils

## Ingredients

- 1.5 cups lentils, rinsed
- 1/2 large onion, diced
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp red curry paste
- 1/2 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- A few shakes of cayenne
- 1 (14 oz) can tomato puree
- 1/4 cup coconut milk/cream
- Cilantro, for garnish
- Rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. Cook lentils according to package. Drain and set aside.
2. Melt butter over medium-high heat in a saucepan.
3. Add onion and sauté until fragrant and golden brown (a few minutes).
4. Add all spices and stir fry for 1-2 minutes.
5. Add tomato puree and simmer until smooth.
6. Add lentils and coconut milk/cream. Stir to combine.
7. Simmer for 15-20 minutes.
8. Serve over rice, garnished with cilantro.
