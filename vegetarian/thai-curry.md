# Thai Curry

## Ingredients

- 1 (13.5 oz) can coconut milk (do not shake can)
- 1/2 cup vegetable stock
- Grated ginger, to taste (or from a tube)
- Diced garlic, to taste (or from a tube)
- 4 teaspoons soy sauce
- 4 teaspoons palm sugar or brown sugar
- 6 tablespoons (or recommended amount on package) Thai green curry paste, like Thai Taste, Maesri, or Thai Kitchen (none contain shrimp or fish paste)
- 1/2 cup diced (1/2 inch) onion
- About 3 cups chopped veggies/filling, such as:
  - Bamboo shoots
  - Red bell pepper
  - Zucchini/other squash
  - Yukon gold potatoes (cut into small chunks)
  - Green beans
  - Broccoli
  - Eggplant
- 1 lime
- Basil leaves, for garnish
- Jasmine rice (optional)

## Instructions

1. Start rice, if desired.
2. Heat a pan over medium heat and fry the chopped onions in oil.
3. While onions cook, combine the coconut milk, vegetable stock, soy sauce, and sugar. Stir until the sugar is dissolved.
4. After the onions have cooked a few minutes, add green curry paste and garlic/ginger to taste. Cook a few minutes, stirring to keep the garlic from burning.
5. Add the coconut milk mixture (and potatoes, if using). Bring to a simmer.
6. If using potatoes, simmer about 15 minutes with just the potatoes and onions before adding the rest of the vegetables. Otherwise, add vegetables when the simmering begins.
7. Simmer about 15 minutes, until vegetables are cooked to your liking.
8. Juice the lime into the curry.
9. Spice to taste and serve with basil to garnish.
