# Stir-Fried Tofu

**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound firm tofu
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce, divided
- 1 1/2 teaspoons brown sugar, divided
- 2 tablespoons frying oil, divided
- 2 teaspoons hoisin sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 2 red bell peppers, seeded and cut in 1-inch squares
- 1 green bell pepper, seeded and cut in 1-inch squares
- 1 tablespoon minced fresh ginger
- 2 large garlic cloves, green shoots removed, minced
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon dried red pepper flakes (to taste)
- 2 scallions, white and green parts, cut on the diagonal into 1-inch lengths
- Cooked rice or noodles, for serving

## Instructions

1. Blot the tofu dry, wrap in a clean kitchen towel, and place a cutting board on top. Let sit about 15 minutes.
2. Slice the tofu about 1/2 inch thick into 1x2-inch dominoes.
3. In a medium bowl, mix 1 tbsp of the soy sauce, 1 tsp of the brown sugar, and 1 tbsp of the oil. Toss with the tofu, making sure all pieces are coated.
4. Let sit 5 to 10 minutes while you prepare the other ingredients.
5. Start heating a cast iron pan.
6. In a small bowl, stir together the remaining soy sauce, remaining sugar, hoisin sauce, and sesame oil.
7. When the cast iron is hot, add the remaining oil, then the peppers.
8. When the peppers begin to soften, add the garlic and ginger. Stir-fry 20 seconds.
9. Add the tofu, red pepper flakes, and scallions. Stir-fry 2 minutes.
10. Stir and add the sauce. Cover and cook 3 minutes.
11. Stir, taste, and adjust seasonings. Serve with rice or noodles.
