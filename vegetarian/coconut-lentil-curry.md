# Coconut Lentil Curry

## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons oil
- 1 tbsp cumin seed
- 1 tbsp coriander seed
- 1 head of garlic, chopped (10-12 cloves)
- 1 (15 oz) can coconut milk
- A few handfuls cherry tomatoes
- 1 cup chopped cilantro

### Lentil Mix (can be premixed)

- 1 cup dried brown lentils (or mung beans)
- 2 teaspoons cayenne
- 3 cups water

### Tomato Mix (can be premixed)

- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp ginger
- 1 tbsp turmeric
- 2 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Heat oil, then toast cumin and coriander seeds for ~45 seconds, until they begin to brown.
2. Add garlic and let it brown, ~2 minutes.
3. Add the tomato mix (crushed tomatoes, ginger, turmeric, and salt).
4. Cook ~5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Add the lentil mix (lentils, cayenne, and 3 cups water).
6. Reduce heat to low and simmer 35-40 minutes, or until lentils are soft, stirring occasionally to keep lentils from sticking. Add water if the curry looks dry.
7. Once lentils are soft, add coconut milk and cherry tomatoes. Return to a simmer.
8. Remove the pot from heat.
9. Stir in the cilantro.
