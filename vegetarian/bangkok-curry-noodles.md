# Bangkok Curry Noodles

Make 4 servings of the sauce to use across two nights, and 2 fresh bowls each night.

## Ingredients

### Coconut Curry Sauce (4 servings)

- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 shallots
- 1 tbsp fresh minced ginger, or paste
- 2 tbsp red curry paste
- 1 (14 oz) can coconut milk
- 1/2 cup veggie broth
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 3 tbsp hot chili paste
- 4 tbsp soy sauce

### Bowls (2 servings)

- 4 ounces rice noodles
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1/2 onion, chopped
- 2/3 cup chopped broccoli florets
- 2/3 cup shredded carrots
- 2/3 cup chopped asparagus
- 2/3 cup shredded purple cabbage
- Sesame seeds, for topping
- Limes, for serving
- A handful of fresh basil, for serving

## Instructions

1. Soak the noodles in a bowl of cold water. Soak time depends on noodle thickness; thick noodles need 20+ minutes in warm water.

### Sauce

2. Stir fry shallots and ginger in oil for 3-5 minutes.
3. Add curry paste; stir fry for 1 minute.
4. Add the coconut milk, sugar, chili paste, soy sauce, and vegetable broth.
5. Simmer for ~15 minutes while prepping ingredients for the bowls.

### Bowls

6. In a large skillet, heat the oil over high heat.
7. Add the onion, carrots, broccoli, and asparagus.
8. Stir fry for ~5 minutes until the broccoli and asparagus are bright green.
9. Add the noodles and toss with the vegetables.
10. Add the sauce and toss until just combined (cooking too long at this point can make the noodles sticky).
11. Serve topped with the purple cabbage and sesame seeds, with a squeeze of lime and basil leaves.
