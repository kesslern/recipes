# Paella

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1 (8 oz) jar roasted red peppers, drained and chopped
- 2 red peppers, seeded and chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 medium zucchini, chopped
- 3 large tomatoes, seeded and chopped
- 1 pinch saffron threads
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 1/2 cups short-grain white rice
- 3 cups vegetable broth
- Pinch of salt (depends on how salty your broth is)
- 1/2 cup parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, sliced

## Instructions

1. In a large, shallow sauté pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the onion, red pepper, and roasted red pepper and let brown and reduce, about 10 minutes.
2. Add the zucchini, garlic, tomato, saffron, paprika, and cayenne and cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, another 10 minutes until vegetables are soft.
3. Add the rice and vegetable broth and stir once. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes. Don't touch! Stirring is for risotto, not paella.
4. Top with chopped parsley and sliced lemons.
