# Lemon Vinaigrette

## Ingredients

* ⅓ cup olive oil
* ¼ cup lemon juice
* 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
* ½ teaspoon honey or maple syrup
* 1 garlic clove, minced
* Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Add the olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, honey or maple syrup, minced garlic, salt, and pepper to a small bowl.
2. Whisk together until fully emulsified. Alternatively, add all ingredients to a small jar and shake well to combine.
3. Use immediately, or refrigerate in an airtight container until ready to use.

## Notes

* Store in the refrigerator for up to 1 week in a sealed container.
* If the oil separates, shake or stir well before using.
* If the oil hardens slightly after chilling, let the vinaigrette sit at room temperature for a few minutes before serving.
* For a creamier dressing, try adding tahini.
* To make this dressing Whole30 compliant, omit the honey.
