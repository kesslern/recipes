# Sausage Gravy

## Ingredients

- 1 lb sausage
- 3 cups milk
- 1/2 cup flour
- Salt
- Pepper

## Instructions

1. Brown sausage.
2. While sausage is browning, heat about 3 cups milk in the microwave to scalding at most. Don't let it boil.
3. If you want the gravy to be beige, get a good brown on some of the meat so you can scrape the brown up later.
4. Break sausage up into desired texture; sometimes I make it more coarse with larger chunks.
5. When meat is browned, turn heat down to medium-low and stir in about 1/2 cup flour.
6. Stir flour and sausage for about a minute. If you don't cook it a bit, it can taste like glue. Be sure to scrape up the browned bits.
7. Pour in most of the hot milk while stirring. Turn up heat so it just comes to a boil, but don't let it boil to death. If the milk is hot, it won't take long. Add about 1/4 tsp salt and pepper.
8. If it's too thick, add more milk, about 2 tbsp at a time if making a single batch.

## Notes

- It will thicken after you refrigerate it, so you might want to add a bit of milk to leftovers.
- Use a good-quality sausage.
- Do not drain the fat after browning; it's needed to interact with the flour properly to make gravy.
- Be sure to serve with real baking-powder biscuits, not biscuits from a tube.
