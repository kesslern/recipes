# Beef Fried Rice

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons cooking oil, divided
- 2 eggs, whisked
- 8 oz flank steak, cubed
- 2 medium carrots, diced
- 1 small white onion, diced
- 1/2 cup frozen peas
- 3 green onions, thinly sliced
- 1 cup broccoli, cut into small florets
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups cooked and chilled rice (white or brown)
- 3 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a large skillet.
2. Add the eggs and scramble until fully cooked. Remove from the pan and set aside.
3. Add the remaining tablespoon of oil to the pan.
4. Add the steak and cook over high heat for 2-3 minutes, just until seared and caramelized on the outside. Remove from the pan and set aside.
5. Add carrots and broccoli to the pan and cook until tender, 3-4 minutes.
6. Stir in garlic and cook an additional minute.
7. Add the cold rice and sauté 3-4 minutes. The rice should brown up a bit.
8. Add the eggs and steak back to the pan and stir in soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil. Cook 1-2 minutes to heat through.
9. Serve immediately, garnished with sesame seeds if desired.

## Notes

The source lists onion, peas, and green onions in the ingredients but never uses them in the steps. Add the onion with the carrots, the peas with the rice, and the green onions as a garnish.
