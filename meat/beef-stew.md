# Beef Stew

## Ingredients

- Prime chuck roast
- Yellow onion
- 2-3 carrots
- Potatoes
- Garlic
- 96-128 oz beef stock
- Tomato paste
- 1 tbsp flour
- 1 cup frozen cut green beans
- Bay leaf
- 28 oz diced tomatoes
- 1/2 tsp ground star anise
- Red wine (optional)
- Salt and pepper

### Seasoning

Cumin, sage, thyme, cayenne, garlic, mustard, nutmeg (small amounts), soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, coriander, and sometimes dill. Oregano, rosemary, or basil sometimes make their way in as well.

## Instructions

### Preparation

1. Cut chuck roast into 1/2-1" cubes.
2. Dice carrots and onion.
3. Peel and dice potatoes.
4. Grind 1/2 teaspoon of star anise.

### Cooking

5. Sear and brown each piece of chuck roast in a hot cast iron pan, a few at a time to keep the temperature up.
6. Transfer the fat to a large pot and cook onions, carrots, and tomato paste over medium heat until the onions turn translucent.
7. Add and cook garlic, then add flour and brown it.
8. If using red wine, add 1/4 to 1/2 cup to deglaze, then reduce.
9. Add beef stock, bay leaf, green beans, diced tomatoes, beef cubes, star anise, salt, pepper, and other seasoning.
10. Bring to a boil, then simmer.
11. Add diced potatoes 1 hour before eating.

## Notes

- Add dark chocolate powder or coffee (grounds or leftover liquid) for a hearty, earthy character.
- Avoid chili powder so it doesn't turn into chili.
