# Fried Fish

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, divided (1/2 cup and 1 cup)
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tablespoon salt, divided (1 teaspoon and 2 teaspoons)
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 cup beer (lager or ale)
- Cold water, as needed
- 1 1/2 pounds skinless cod fillets (or haddock), cut to preferred size
- Kosher salt
- Lemon wedges

## Instructions

1. Preheat oil to 325°F.
2. In a shallow bowl or dish, mix 1/2 cup flour, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1 teaspoon black pepper.
3. In a separate bowl, combine 1 cup flour, 2 teaspoons salt, and 1 teaspoon garlic powder.
4. Gradually whisk the egg and 1 cup beer into the second bowl until smooth. The batter should be the consistency of thick cream.
   - If too thick, stream in cold water 1 tablespoon at a time.
   - If too thin, sprinkle in more flour 1 teaspoon at a time.
5. Pre-fry french fries for ~3 minutes.
6. Increase oil temperature to 375°F.
7. Dredge fish in the dry flour mixture and tap off the excess. Dip in the beer batter and let the excess drain off.
8. Working in batches, carefully add battered fish and the pre-fried chips to the oil and cook until golden brown, about 5-7 minutes.
9. Transfer to a paper towel-lined baking sheet or cooling rack. Sprinkle with kosher salt while still warm.

## Notes

Serve with coleslaw and/or tartar sauce.
