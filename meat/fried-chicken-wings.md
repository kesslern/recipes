# Fried Chicken Wings

## Ingredients

Breading for 12 wings, mixed in a gallon bag:

- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1 tablespoon ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tsp ground smoked habanero

## Instructions

1. Rinse chicken in cold water and pat dry.
2. Bread the wings in the bag.
3. Fry in oil at 375°F, 6 at a time, for 8-10 minutes.
