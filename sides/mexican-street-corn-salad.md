# 🌽 Mexican Street Corn Salad

A vibrant, creamy, and flavorful twist on the classic elote (Mexican street corn), served as a salad instead of on the cob. Perfect as a side dish for grills, potlucks, or weeknight dinners.

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes
**Total Time:** 25 minutes
**Servings:** 4

---

## Ingredients

### For the Corn

- 4 ears of fresh corn, husked and silk removed
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper, to taste

### For the Dressing & Mix-Ins

- ½ cup sour cream
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- ¼ cup fresh lime juice (about 2–3 limes)
- 1 teaspoon chili powder (adjust to taste)
- ½ teaspoon ground cumin
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup crumbled cotija cheese (or feta as a substitute)
- ½ cup fresh cilantro, finely chopped
- 1–2 jalapeños, seeded and diced (optional)
- 1 avocado, diced (optional)
- 1 small red onion, finely diced (optional)

### For Garnish

- Extra crumbled cotija cheese
- Fresh cilantro leaves
- Lime wedges
- Additional chili powder or Tajín seasoning

---

## Instructions

1. **Char the corn:** Preheat a grill or grill pan over medium-high heat. Brush the corn ears lightly with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Grill for 8–10 minutes, turning occasionally, until lightly charred and tender. Let cool, then cut the kernels off the cobs.

2. **Make the dressing:** In a large bowl, whisk together the sour cream, mayonnaise, lime juice, chili powder, cumin, and minced garlic until smooth.

3. **Combine:** Add the grilled corn kernels to the dressing and toss to coat evenly. Stir in the crumbled cotija cheese, chopped cilantro, jalapeños, avocado, and red onion.

4. **Season and serve:** Taste and adjust seasoning with additional salt, pepper, or lime juice as needed. Transfer to a serving bowl and garnish with extra cotija cheese, cilantro, lime wedges, and a sprinkle of chili powder or Tajín.

5. **Chill (optional):** For best flavor, refrigerate for 20–30 minutes before serving to allow the flavors to meld.

---

## Notes

- **Fresh vs. frozen corn:** Fresh grilled corn is ideal, but you can substitute 4 cups of thawed frozen corn or canned corn (drained) for a quicker version.
- **Cotija cheese:** If cotija is unavailable, feta cheese or queso fresco are great substitutes.
- **Make ahead:** This salad tastes even better the next day. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
- **Spice level:** Adjust the jalapeños and chili powder to your preferred heat level.
