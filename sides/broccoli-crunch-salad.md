# Broccoli Crunch Salad

## Ingredients

* 1 cup mayonnaise
* 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
* 3 tablespoons white sugar
* 1 teaspoon onion powder
* 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
* Salt to taste
* 4 cups broccoli florets, broken into bite-sized pieces
* 1 (12 ounce) bag broccoli slaw mix
* 1/2 cup sunflower seeds
* 1/2 cup dried cranberries
* 1/2 cup chopped red onion (optional)
* 1 cup chopped cooked bacon

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine the mayonnaise, apple cider vinegar, sugar, onion powder, black pepper, and salt. Whisk until smooth and creamy. Set aside.
2. In a large bowl, combine the broccoli slaw mix, broccoli florets, red onion, sunflower seeds, dried cranberries, and bacon.
3. Pour the dressing over the broccoli mixture and toss until everything is evenly coated.
4. Chill until ready to serve.

## Notes

* Prep Time: 15 minutes
* Total Time: 15 minutes
* Servings: 8
* For best flavor, refrigerate the salad for at least 30 minutes before serving.
