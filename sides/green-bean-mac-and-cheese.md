# Green Bean Mac and Cheese

This green bean mac and cheese is a mashup of two favorites, green bean casserole and macaroni and cheese. Topped with crunchy French-fried onions, it is everything you could hope for. The green beans add texture, gorgeous color, and just a hint of delicious bitterness to amplify all the other flavors.

**Prep Time:** 15 mins
**Cook Time:** 45 mins
**Stand Time:** 10 mins
**Total Time:** 1 hr 10 mins
**Servings:** 10

---

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons salt
- 1 pound green beans, trimmed
- 1 pound elbow macaroni
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 5 cups cold milk
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
- 1 pinokuch ground nutmeg
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1/4 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1 pound extra sharp Cheddar cheese, freshly grated
- 1 cup French fried onions, or more as needed

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Bring 3 quarts of generously salted water to a boil over high heat. Add beans and cook until they just start to get tender but are not soft, about 4 minutes. Remove with a hand strainer to a bowl; set aside until cool enough to handle. Cut into 1-inch pieces and reserve.
3. Add elbow macaroni to the boiling bean water and cook 1 minute less than package directions, about 8 minutes. Transfer into a colander and let drain for 1 minute, then transfer into a large bowl.
4. For best results, make the cheese sauce while the pasta is cooking: melt butter over medium heat. Whisk in flour and cook for a few minutes to take the raw flavor away from the flour. Add cold milk all at once and whisk to combine. Continue cooking, stirring occasionally, until the mixture comes to a simmer and thickens (heat can be raised to medium-high to save time, if you stir constantly). While the sauce is thickening, stir in salt, cayenne, thyme, white pepper, nutmeg, Dijon mustard, and Worcestershire sauce.
5. Once the mixture is thick, turn off heat and stir in cheese until melted. Add green beans to the hot, drained macaroni, pour in cheese sauce, and mix to combine evenly. Transfer into a 9x13-inch baking dish. Place on a sheet pan to catch any drips.
6. Scatter the surface evenly with fried onions and press them lightly into the surface with your fingertips.
7. Bake in the preheated oven until the mac and cheese is hot and the top is browned, about 30 minutes. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- **Chef's Note:** Pre-grated cheese is covered in a cellulose dust, which can result in an off texture. Grate cheese freshly from a block, just before use, for a nice, creamy sauce.
