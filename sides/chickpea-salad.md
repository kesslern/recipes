# Chickpea Salad

## Ingredients

* 2 (15-ounce) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
* 1 large cucumber, diced
* 1 red bell pepper, diced
* 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
* ¼ cup red onion, diced
* 4 ounces feta cheese, crumbled
* ¼ cup finely chopped parsley
* Lemon vinaigrette, to taste

## Instructions

1. Add the chickpeas, cucumber, red bell pepper, cherry tomatoes, red onion, feta cheese, and parsley to a large mixing bowl.
2. Pour the lemon vinaigrette over the salad.
3. Gently stir until all ingredients are well combined.
4. Serve immediately or chill before serving.

## Notes

* Use as much or as little lemon vinaigrette as you prefer.
* This salad can be made ahead and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
* For extra flavor, add olives or avocado before serving.
